Muffin is a **cute predator**: a Class III [meta-dimensional entity](/star-rangers/lore/predatory-entities/), non-sapient, of the type the classification calls a *lamb-mask*. It does not plan. It has no goals, no strategy, and no model of anyone it feeds on. It occupies a niche the way damp occupies a wall.

It sits by the Deck 7 galley door where the warm air comes out. It is small. It is, by universal agreement, adorable. Beyond that the descriptions do not agree with one another and never have: eleven staff statements taken by [Detective Inspector Albercombe](/star-rangers/characters/wendell-albercombe/)'s office over one shift returned a cat, a rabbit, a very young dog, and "you know — Muffin," and the four people who said cat did not agree about the colour. Nobody found this strange at the time. That is the first symptom, and it presents before any of the others.

The name is not the entity's. Eden's staff supplied it, the way people do, and it is doing more work than any of them realise. There is no procedure on this habitat by which a compliance-framework hazard report gets filed against something called Muffin, and everyone who has considered writing one has discovered this for themselves, privately, and put the form down.

## What It Actually Does

A lamb-mask feeds on micro-consent collapses: the small yes given because no would have been rude. Muffin does not take food. It is *given* food, all day, by people who did not mean to and would have said they were only being nice.

What accumulates is not the feeding. It is the habit. A deck that has spent nine months practising the small unearned yes gets better at it, and does not confine the skill to the galley door. [Superintendent Oyelaran's](/star-rangers/characters/rasa-oyelaran/) bureau ran the numbers sideways, on a hunch that was not hers: over the same nine months, Deck 7's monitoring-officer sign-offs went from a median of two returned-for-correction per cycle to none at all, on a caseload that had not changed and a compliance framework that had grown by two more requirements. Nothing was falsified. Nothing was even wrong, so far as anyone could show. A deck's worth of people had simply, gradually, stopped being the kind of people who send a form back.

This is what the classification means by *normalised boundary erosion*, and it is why the entry insists the damage is cumulative rather than acute. Nobody on Deck 7 was harmed. Everybody on Deck 7 got easier to ask.

## Why the Smart Pets

The lore is blunt about it: robots and cyber-enhanced animals of the detective agency detect cute predators early, because their asymmetry detection is not distorted by social pressure to be polite. Eden is the worked case.

[Agent Barsik](/star-rangers/characters/agent-barsik/) has never given Muffin anything. Neither has [Bubochka](/star-rangers/characters/bubochka/), who was assigned the galley approach as an observation exercise before anyone had a reason to think it mattered, and whose log is now the only continuous record of the thing that exists. [Thrum](/star-rangers/characters/thrum/) sits four metres away in the service void and has never once registered that Muffin is there at all, which is not vigilance — a vessik has no apparatus for perceiving charm, so there is nothing for a lamb-mask to work with and no interaction to record.

Muffin does not avoid them. It cannot: avoidance would be strategy, and there is nobody in there to have any. It simply gets nothing from them, so nothing happens, and it goes on getting a great deal from everyone else. The three of them are not resisting it. They are a control group.

## The Difficulty

Everything the agency has is a negative. Its case is that four residents of one deck have never fed the entity, that one of the four is an animal with no social perception whatsoever, and that the sign-off numbers moved. None of that is a hazard finding, and none of it can be written on a form without the form containing the word *Muffin*.

Barsik's position, filed once and not repeated, is that the counter is not force and not a better report. It is the same counter the classification gives for the whole class: somebody has to refuse it, out loud, in front of the others, and be the sort of person who can stand being rude for four seconds. He has noted that this is a great deal harder to arrange on Eden than a warrant.
