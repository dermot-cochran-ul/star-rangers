A **formtype** is one of the lawful templates a Formwright instantiates: a structure built out of its [Metawright's](/star-rangers/lore/cosmic-cascade/) mathematics, settling what forms are available to be built out of at all. The universes descending from one Formwright's formtypes constitute its [formcluster](/star-rangers/glossary/formcluster/).

**A formtype is not an archetype**, and the two are confused constantly, both being templates and neither being a thing. An [archecluster's](/star-rangers/glossary/archecluster/) archetypes are a *vocabulary*; formtypes are the *grammar that permits it*. Archewrights assemble archetypes and possibility space from whatever the formtypes admit — which is why two Archewrights working one formcluster can produce archetype sets with nothing recognisable in common, and why neither could have assembled the other's under a different Formwright's templates.

**Nor is it a physical law.** A universe's physics envelope, Etheric coupling and interaction regime are the [Worldwright's](/star-rangers/glossary/worldwright/) work, three tiers below. A formtype does not say what happens, or even what may happen. It settles what shape a thing that happens is permitted to have.

**The largest formtype is the [Common Manifold](/star-rangers/lore/post-eleven-dimensional-manifold/)** — the arena every membrane in the cluster is seated in. The three tiers are visible in that one object: [Conseleme](/star-rangers/glossary/conseleme/) fixes that seating must obtain, a Metawright supplies mathematics a manifold could be expressed in, and the Formwright instantiates the manifold satisfying the first in the terms of the second. This is why a formcluster is a common *seating* as well as a common origin, and why two universes in different formclusters have no measured separation at all — no shared arena exists to measure it across.

**The Archive uses the word sparingly and says why.** Nothing at Material scale has ever observed a formtype, and nothing ever will: an instrument is a thing built inside the grammar and cannot be turned on it. What the record holds is the inference — that archetype sets recur across universes with no contact and no common Archewright, in ways a shared vocabulary does not explain and a shared grammar does. Survey Corps files this under structure rather than under observation, and its own manual notes that a term one can neither measure nor do without is exactly the term to keep an eye on.

**Example:** A comparative analyst maps two archecluster's archetype sets against each other and finds no correspondence at all — not a rough one, not a partial one. They share a formcluster nonetheless, and the Archive's finding is the unglamorous one: the sets are unrelated in content and identical in what they were permitted to be. Nothing in either could have been assembled under another Formwright's templates. That is the whole of the relationship, and the only part of it any instrument reached.
