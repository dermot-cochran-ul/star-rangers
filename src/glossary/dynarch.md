A **Dynarch** (pronounced: **DY-nark**; plural: **Dynarchs**; from *dynamis*, force) — commonly also called an **Exarch**, the older form still preferred in devotional and provincial records, and both names are canonical — is a Cascade-tier being that encapsulates and governs a physical law or force — **Gravity**, **Electromagnetics**, or **Nature** (biological life), among others. Each Dynarch embodies one force-domain: it is not a mind that operates the force from outside, but the tier at which that force is lawfully administered at all. "Exarch" carries the office's character precisely: a governor holding a province on delegated authority from a distant throne.

## A Dynarch Is a Proxy Telearch

The Dynarchs are not merely *aligned* with the [Telearch](/star-rangers/glossary/telearch/) line. **A Dynarch is a Telearch acting by proxy inside a universe, scoped to a single force-domain.** Telearchs are native to no universe and supervise from outside; a force has to be administered from within the thing it runs through. The Dynarch tier is how that is done.

This is what the tier's other properties are consequences of, rather than rules laid on top of it:

- **Why continuity prevails on conflict.** Where a zone's local preference and cross-boundary continuity disagree, continuity wins — not by an arbitration rule imposed from above, but because continuity is what a Dynarch substantially *is*. A proxy cannot be made to rule against the office it proxies for.
- **Why they are co-created.** Both branches of the Cascade matrix genuinely contribute: the Telearch line supplies what a Dynarch *is*, and the [Concordant Principal](/star-rangers/glossary/concordant-principal/) line supplies the zone-scoping that gives it somewhere to act. Co-creation is not two parents of equal kind — it is substance from one line and jurisdiction from the other.
- **Why the dual obligation is not a contradiction.** Zone-local operation of a force runs with the zone's Principal; continuity of that force across boundaries and universes runs up the Telearch line. A proxy answering to the local office for local matters, and to its own principal for everything else, is an ordinary arrangement rather than a divided loyalty.

**Not an avatar.** The Cascade's other instrument for acting from inside — the [incarnated avatar](/star-rangers/lore/glossary/canonical-glossary-and-migration-guide/) — crosses a temporal register, appearing at Celestial level or below, immanent in Material time and a separate limited person from its source. A Dynarch does none of that: it stays in conceptual time, above the Celestials, and is a standing office rather than a person sent down. Proxy and avatar are two different answers to the same problem, at two different depths.

Dynarchs rank between Concordant Principals (Harmonarchs) and [Celestials](/star-rangers/glossary/celestials/). In the Cascade, higher strata create and define the layers beneath them more than they command them — a Dynarch's dual obligation is written into what it *is*, not imposed on it order by order.

**Key constraints:**
- Dynarchs govern the *operation* of a force, not its design: the rule-sets are the Worldwright's, and a Dynarch cannot rewrite a zone's physics.
- Dual obligation to Principal and Telearch; where zone-local preference and cross-boundary continuity conflict, continuity prevails.
- Dynarchs do not perform enforcement actions; rule-integrity intervention remains Celestial work.

**Legacy usage:** older records call this tier **higher celestials** — a term also used loosely for Telearchs (as "High Celestials"). The ambiguity is why both usages were retired; see [High Celestials](/star-rangers/glossary/high-celestials/) for the disambiguation rule.

Dynarchs are also the lowest stratum of the Cascade's **conceptual time**: above the [Celestials](/star-rangers/glossary/celestials/), existence runs in meta-time — real sequence, real before-and-after, but not the Material layer's clock — and true eternity begins higher still, above the Worldwrights. A Dynarch stands just across the temporal threshold from the world whose forces it holds.

**Example:** Gravity does not fail in a boundary zone, even where timebases disagree and instruments quarrel — the force stays administered across the seam. That cross-boundary coherence is Dynarch work, and it is also why low-context observers have worshipped this tier more than any other: a being that *is* the storm, the tide, or the living green — and that no Material clock touches — reads as a god to anyone who cannot see the ladder it stands on.
