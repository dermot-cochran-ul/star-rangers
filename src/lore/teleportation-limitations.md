A legal jump is over before a witness can finish a breath, but the body remembers it longer. That aftershock is part of the rule, not an accident.

Teleportation in Star Rangers canon is not magic and not unconstrained instant movement.
It is a short-range harmonic relocation event that depends on the same lawful field behavior as **Quantum Space Harmonic Wave** transit.

## Core Limitation Set

Every legal teleportation event requires all of the following:

1. **Direct line of sight** between origin operator and target endpoint.
2. **Local quantum-space harmonic coherence** sufficient to hold a relocation lock.
3. **High available power reserve** to drive the relocation pulse.

If any one of these fails, teleportation must be treated as unsafe and non-executable.

## Why Line of Sight Is Mandatory

Teleportation lock is built from observed endpoint geometry, not inferred geometry.
Opaque barriers, heavy particulate fields, or active harmonic distortion break endpoint certainty and invalidate lock quality.

Operationally, this means:

- No legal blind jumps through walls or around corners.
- No legal jump to stale sensor-only coordinates without visual confirmation.
- No legal jump into boundary ambiguity zones without a verified visual lane.

The rule reads as an abstraction until somebody has to satisfy it on a clock. In [Sightline Window (S01E02C04)](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e02/s01e02c04/) a hull-fitter is sealed on the wrong side of a containment door with under three minutes of useful consciousness and a six-minute walking route, and the entire jump turns on whether Dr. Iona Vale can find a true optical lane to an endpoint she is not willing to place a body into unseen. She finds one through an emergency inspection port that six years of maintenance logs had carried as decorative. Particle occlusion crossing that lane cost fourteen per cent of lock confidence in real time — which is what "narrow, unstable" means when it is written down afterward as a tolerance figure.

## Physiological and Clinical Cost

Reported transit sensation is commonly described as similar to **electroconvulsive therapy**:
brief, disorienting, and neurologically intense even when the jump is technically successful.

Canonical medical risks increase with repeated or poorly timed teleport events:

- Elevated risk of **anxiety** symptoms.
- Elevated risk of **depression** symptoms.
- Increased likelihood of **central fatigue** and post-jump cognitive slowing.

These effects are measurable, cumulative, and require enforced recovery protocols in formal operations.

A minority of jumps executed at or near active boundary zones produce a distinct clinical picture the Medical Corps tracks separately: see [Post-Teleport Ascension Stress Disorder](/star-rangers/lore/post-teleport-ascension-stress-disorder/).

## Energy Economics

Teleportation is energetically expensive compared with ordinary local movement.
The relocation pulse requires a large short-duration power draw to force coherent matter-state remapping through the local harmonic field.

For this reason:

- Teleportation is reserved for high-value tactical or rescue cases.
- Stations throttle jump frequency to protect grid stability.
- Teams must budget power for life-support and shielding before authorizing teleport events.

The order of magnitude is on record: the Threshold rescue pulse drew twenty-two per cent of local ring reserve in 1.8 seconds, which is why the honest answer to *how much does this cost us* was that life support would notice a second one. A station does not ration jumps because the draw is theoretically large. It rations them because the draw competes, in the same minute, with the systems keeping everyone else breathing.

## Continuity Constraints

- Teleportation always relies on quantum space harmonics; there is no separate mystical teleport mechanism.
- Stories may show disagreement about policy, but not about these physical constraints.
- Any chapter featuring teleport use must identify sightline status, energy state, and post-jump condition.

## In the Record

The three-condition rule above is not a background regulation the stories inherited; [The Harmonic Price (S01E02C05)](/star-rangers/seasons/s01/e02/s01e02c05/) is the room where it gets written, hours after the rescue that made writing it unavoidable — and the argument that shapes it is about whether to include an exception clause. Thorne, who had just authorised a jump on a thin margin, wanted one on the grounds that a rule nobody will keep under pressure protects only its author. He was talked out of it by the observation that a clause excusing him in advance is exactly what stops a good officer from feeling the weight of the call. The published constraint set has no exception clause for that reason.
