# Contributing

Short version: **fork it freely, but don't send it back here.**

*Star Rangers* is written and maintained by one person. This file exists so nobody spends an evening on something that was never going to be merged, and so it's clear up front what this project can and can't sustain. None of it is a complaint about anyone's work — it's a statement of capacity.

## Pull requests aren't accepted

**Third-party pull requests are not accepted, including small ones** — typo fixes, dependency bumps, refactors, new themes, new content. Please don't open one; it will be closed unmerged, and that's a worse outcome for you than not writing it.

Two reasons, both structural rather than personal:

- **Creative control.** This is a single-authored novel. Like most authors, I keep authorial control of my own work; a shared writing collaboration would cost me the focus the story runs on. That applies to the prose most obviously, but the engine and the story are shaped by the same set of decisions.
- **A merge is a commitment, not a moment.** Anything merged here is something I've taken on permanently: reviewing it, keeping it working, and answering for it later. That obligation scales with contributions, and my time doesn't.

## Forks are welcome, and unsupported

Forking is explicitly encouraged, and needs no permission from me:

- The **engine** is MIT ([`LICENSE`](./LICENSE)) — take it and build something else entirely.
- The **story content** is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 with an explicit **Fan Works Policy** ([`CONTENT-LICENSE.md`](./CONTENT-LICENSE.md)) permitting non-commercial fan fiction, fan art, and fan-fiction clones of this site.

[`FORKING.md`](./FORKING.md) is the step-by-step guide for both paths.

The condition is the same one that makes the permission possible: **a fork must not create work or obligations here.** I can't review, debug, support, or answer for a fork's code, content, deployment, or hosting. If your fork breaks, it's yours to fix — please don't open an issue here about it, and don't route your users to me for support.

Please also don't present a fork in a way that implies it's official, endorsed by me, or written by me — the licences set the formal terms, and the Fan Works Policy is specific about labeling.

## Issues

Issues on **this** repo are for concrete, checkable problems with **this** site or codebase: a broken link, a build failure, a page that renders wrong, a factual inconsistency in published content. Those are useful, and a short report with a URL is enough.

Everything else — feature requests, structural suggestions, "you should add X", requests for roadmap or timelines — I read but generally won't action, and may close without discussion. Not a judgement on the idea; it's the same capacity limit as above.

## Questions from readers

**Genuine questions from readers and fans are welcome.** Every character, lore, glossary, codex, chapter and journal page carries a comment thread, and the comments repo has open **General**, **Q&A**, **Theories & Predictions**, and **Fan Creations** categories — see the [Discussion forum](./README.md#discussion-forum-giscus) section of the README. That's the right place to ask; a public answer helps the next reader too.

What I can't promise is coverage. I triage, I answer what I can, and I answer some things slowly or not at all. **Silence is capacity, not dismissal.** Asking once in the right place is more likely to get an answer than asking in several.

There is no social-media presence for this project — I don't use social media, and no account anywhere speaks for it. This repo, the site, and the comments board are the whole of it.

## In short

Take the engine, write your own story, write fan work under the Fan Works Policy — all encouraged. Just keep it self-contained, so this stays something one person can actually sustain.
