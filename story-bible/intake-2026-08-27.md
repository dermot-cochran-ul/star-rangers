## Canon check

**The published record already shows this division of labour, without naming
it.** The direction reads less like new material than like the explanation the
Dock Seven chapter was already carrying:

- **Tobble's disciplined, scientific subsystem is on the page in everything he
  does.** At Dock Seven he is procedure while everything else is coming
  apart: *"Do the arithmetic again"* (`s05e02c03`, on duty, when she offers
  him the shuttle); *"Yes. Hold still."* (as she fragments); and then the
  preservation itself — finding "the one part of it that is still standing,"
  putting "something small and living inside that part," on the lost vessel's
  own archival stores. `s06e01c01`'s canon_facts bind the mechanism:
  transference "depends on" a substrate "in scaffold-dependent conditions,"
  and the interior "was preserved by Tobble rather than generated by the
  stall." Preservation onto archival substrate under collapse conditions is
  archival science done under fire. A disciplined deeper subsystem is an
  **extension** in the canon-stability sense — and of the best kind, because
  it explains behaviour the chapters already bind rather than adding beside
  them.
- **The RPG structure is a concrete high-IWD architecture.** IWD's own
  worked example — the mind that "maintains several nested models of itself
  at once and can sit with an unresolved paradox without collapsing it" — is
  the RPG-structured interior described abstractly. Two distinct structural
  idioms in one system (her high-fantasy layer, his disciplined one) is
  "self-model multiplicity" made vivid, and retroactively grounds why this
  system in particular carried the noöseed viability the S6–7 arc turns on.
- **The fantasy-furniture rule transfers inward.** The corpus's signature
  move is "fantasy furniture with specified physics underneath" (CLAUDE.md,
  2026-08-25: the register may be mythic; the mechanism may not be). An inner
  world structured as a high fantasy RPG is that move applied inside a mind:
  the register is entirely hers, and the mechanism underneath is the one the
  glossary already specifies — self-modelling, layered intention, memory
  topology. No licensed deviation is needed.
- **It is character-consistent texture.** She was raised on Tír na nÓg — a
  world named for the Otherworld — on a Boirinn flock-holding six generations
  deep, beside a hillside question the clan kept rules about; she wrote and
  sang for Slipwave before the uniform. A high-fantasy interior register for
  this particular person is the opposite of arbitrary. And the outer person
  the corpus insists on — flat-voiced, will not claim past her evidence — is
  not contradicted but *counterpointed*: the discipline shows on the outside,
  the romance is kept where only the system sees it. That asymmetry is good
  character writing and worth preserving in any realization: the inner world
  is not a disclosure she owes anyone (her own stated posture about the
  system, `tissadelle-shepherd.md`).
- **Boundaries any realization must respect.** (1) The published S6–7
  interior furniture stands: "the two women her inner world keeps closest"
  (`s07e01c02`) are the made Órla and the made Wender, and the preserved
  interior stays pastoral as published — partial preservation is the canon
  fact that makes room. (2) Tone: the RPG frame must not tip the S6 material
  into game-flavoured whimsy (nor give the cognitive-horror premise a
  game-over cruelty); the arc note's "not fantasy content" line remains the
  S6 guard under the reading taken. (3) The plural-minds glossary stays
  general — this is one system's interior, filed on the character page, not a
  new claim about what plural interiors are like.

No conflicts found under the readings above. The direction is an extension —
it names the architecture behind published behaviour and adds an interior
register nothing published forecloses.
