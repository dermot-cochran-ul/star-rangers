# Recurring Themes

## Wisdom Versus Intelligence
- Intelligence solves problems.
- Wisdom chooses which problems are worth solving.
- Knowledge accumulates.
- Discernment is earned.

## The Limits of Virtue
- Every virtue has a liability point.
- Loyalty can become self-sacrifice.
- Patience can become stagnation.
- Humility can become invisibility.
- Perseverance can become stubbornness.

## Finite Energy
- Energy is a more important constraint than time.
- The challenge is not doing everything.
- The challenge is choosing where to invest.

## The Older Hero
- Experience versus youthful strength.
- Judgment versus raw capability.
- Refinement versus growth.
- Legacy versus achievement.

## Mentorship
- Teaching others as a form of heroism.
- Legacy through successors.
- Modeling recovery from mistakes.

## Systems Thinking
- Civilizations as engineered systems.
- Social structures as feedback loops.
- Maintenance versus innovation.

## The Archive and the Memory
- A record remembers in more detail than the person who made it.
- Memory keeps the meaning and loses the particulars.
- The archive keeps the particulars and never knew the meaning.
- Recollection drifts silently. Only a record catches it drifting.
- Preferring your memory to your archive feels like judgment. Usually it is only confidence.
- The one who made the record is not automatically its best reader.
- An archive answers what happened. It cannot answer what it was worth.

## Plurality Beyond the Human Case — unwritten, 28 July 2026

Dermot's note: **alien brains might be even more complex.** The `plural-minds`
glossary currently reasons entirely from the human case — one Conceptual-layer
identity per Material-layer body as the ordinary ratio, plurality as the same
rule with a different count. Nothing in that argument is species-specific, which
means it should already predict alien minds whose ordinary ratio is not one.

Worth deciding before it gets written:

- Does a species exist whose *baseline* is plural, making the single-identity
  human case the unusual one from their side?
- Does the Prismeri two-form lifecycle bear on this? A people who metamorphose
  into a structurally different body is a different question from plurality,
  but the two sit close enough to be confused if neither is stated clearly.
- Tissadelle's own complexity is what qualifies her for a noögenic seed mind.
  If alien minds run more complex still, the qualification is not a human
  distinction, and the seeding system's selection criteria need to say so.

Reference sources for the real-world grounding: morethanone.info (primary) and
pluralpedia.org (secondary). Terminology rules are in the memory note; the short
version is that plurality is never written as pathology, instability, or
spectacle.
