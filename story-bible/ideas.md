# Recurring Themes

## Wisdom Versus Intelligence
- Intelligence solves problems.
- Wisdom chooses which problems are worth solving.
- Knowledge accumulates.
- Discernment is earned.

## The Limits of Virtue
- Every virtue has a liability point.
- Loyalty can become self-sacrifice.
- Patience can become stagnation.
- Humility can become invisibility.
- Perseverance can become stubbornness.

## Finite Energy
- Energy is a more important constraint than time.
- The challenge is not doing everything.
- The challenge is choosing where to invest.

## The Older Hero
- Experience versus youthful strength.
- Judgment versus raw capability.
- Refinement versus growth.
- Legacy versus achievement.

## Mentorship
- Teaching others as a form of heroism.
- Legacy through successors.
- Modeling recovery from mistakes.

## Systems Thinking
- Civilizations as engineered systems.
- Social structures as feedback loops.
- Maintenance versus innovation.
