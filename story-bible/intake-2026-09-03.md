> the child reader tier is visible from the young adult tier, both are visible
> from the general reader tier and all are visible from the contemplative

## What this says, in one line

The four reading tiers nest. Each tier's edition carries everything the tier
below it carries, plus its own material — children ⊂ young adult ⊂ general ⊂
contemplative — and the top of the ladder may add POV scenes to any chapter
that the lower tiers never see.

## What the registry does today, measured against it

`lib/editions.js` narrows each edition to its own thread and nothing else, so
**the chain does not hold at any step at present:**

| Tier | Edition(s) | Carries today | Would carry under the direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| children | `pets` (undercover-pets.com) | thread `undercover-pets`, topic `undercover-pets.com` | unchanged — the bottom of the chain |
| young adult | `starquest` (starquest.site) | thread `orbital-five-o` | + `undercover-pets` |
| general | `sciencefiction` (sciencefiction.site) | threads `founding-era`, `tissadelle-arc`, plus a hand-listed `characters:` cast | + `orbital-five-o`, `undercover-pets`, and the casts those threads carry |
| contemplative | `fellowship`, `fellowship-archive`, `church-space` | private thread `church-space` plus each order's topics | + all four public threads and their casts |

Two things follow that are consequences rather than questions:

- **The general-tier "never" in `edition-reader-profiles.md` gets sharper, not
  softer.** `THREADS` still cannot say "everything except", so a new general-tier
  thread would now need adding to *three* families (general and both
  contemplative). The clean implementation is a tier ladder defined once in
  `lib/editions.js` — `CHILDREN`, `YOUNG_ADULT = CHILDREN + …`, and so on — spread
  into each entry, so the nesting is a property of the file and cannot drift
  entry by entry. `validateEditions` can then assert the chain.
- **Page counts move a long way.** The contemplative editions serve 62–75
  indexable pages today and would serve the whole public corpus plus Season 8,
  roughly 250 or more. The family test in `domain-strategy-and-duplicate-content.md`
  still passes — each tier's page set is a strict superset of the one below, so
  no two families serve the same pages — but the two contemplative families go
  on serving identical page sets, as they already do, and `ranksAt` keeps doing
  its job there.

### What canon already supplies, checked

- **Asteria.** Retired Star Captain, now leads the Fellowship of Light chapter
  on a quiet, unnamed planet; "Asteria the Sage" is the chapter's word. Her
  page has no chapter yet — she appears in no `src/seasons/` file. This would
  be her first scene.
- **The Fellowship's contemplative chapters** are "houses and abbeys that
  trained observers to notice boundary phenomena" and "tend the places where
  the world runs thin" (`fellowship-of-light.md`). A chapter house standing
  near a thin place is the order's ordinary condition, not a coincidence to
  explain.
- **Dreams as a channel** (`dreams-across-the-interval.md`): "Not everything
  that crosses the Interval registers on an instrument. Some of it registers
  on a sleeper." A dreaming mind near a narrowed Interval registers a
  neighbour's bled templates.
- **What a Levril looks like** (`levrils.md`): nothing, on its own account —
  "the perceiver supplies one," and a human meets a figure. Practised minds
  notice faint presences, and "the witnesses likeliest to notice a Levril are,
  by the same disciplines, the witnesses likeliest to have a devotional frame
  ready for what they noticed." *Boundary-aware advisory intervention where
  permitted* is in the permitted column of the Levril table.
- **The 26 August rulings**: Levril interventions "are often interpreted as
  answers to prayer"; an answered prayer or a heard voice has three non-Archive
  homes (Codex, a POV block, the church-space overlay); **no `canon_facts`
  entry may assert that a prayer was answered** — the experience is the fact.
- **What the Record Refuses**: gods who answer, refused outright; prayers are
  not answered by the cosmology. The Archive's position stands.
- **Tone**: unsettling, never horror. Children lost overnight is a fact the
  frame can hold; what the night was like for them is hinted, not shown.

## Closing note, same day — four tiers, open-ended editions

Dermot, verbatim, in two messages:

> I assume that the four reading tiers will be enough to cover most kinds of
> reader

> but more editions could be added as needed

expected to grow**; **editions are open-ended** and are added within a tier as

domains are not enough."* So an edition does not wait on a spare domain: a
subdomain of an existing one is an acceptable home. Recorded for

treats a subdomain as its own host, so nothing in the engine stands in the
way; cPanel would need the subdomain and a clone, which is deploy work, not a
design question.
