# Intake — 3 September 2026

Open questions raised here are indexed in `open-questions.md`, per the standing
convention.

Dermot's direction, given verbatim:

> the child reader tier is visible from the young adult tier, both are visible
> from the general reader tier and all are visible from the contemplative
> edition which may have additional POV scenes for any chapter

## What this says, in one line

The four reading tiers nest. Each tier's edition carries everything the tier
below it carries, plus its own material — children ⊂ young adult ⊂ general ⊂
contemplative — and the top of the ladder may add POV scenes to any chapter
that the lower tiers never see.

## What the registry does today, measured against it

`lib/editions.js` narrows each edition to its own thread and nothing else, so
**the chain does not hold at any step at present:**

| Tier | Edition(s) | Carries today | Would carry under the direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| children | `pets` (undercover-pets.com) | thread `undercover-pets`, topic `undercover-pets.com` | unchanged — the bottom of the chain |
| young adult | `starquest` (starquest.site) | thread `orbital-five-o` | + `undercover-pets` |
| general | `sciencefiction` (sciencefiction.site) | threads `founding-era`, `tissadelle-arc`, plus a hand-listed `characters:` cast | + `orbital-five-o`, `undercover-pets`, and the casts those threads carry |
| contemplative | `fellowship`, `fellowship-archive`, `church-space` | private thread `church-space` plus each order's topics | + all four public threads and their casts |

Two things follow that are consequences rather than questions:

- **The general-tier "never" in `edition-reader-profiles.md` gets sharper, not
  softer.** `THREADS` still cannot say "everything except", so a new general-tier
  thread would now need adding to *three* families (general and both
  contemplative). The clean implementation is a tier ladder defined once in
  `lib/editions.js` — `CHILDREN`, `YOUNG_ADULT = CHILDREN + …`, and so on — spread
  into each entry, so the nesting is a property of the file and cannot drift
  entry by entry. `validateEditions` can then assert the chain.
- **Page counts move a long way.** The contemplative editions serve 62–75
  indexable pages today and would serve the whole public corpus plus Season 8,
  roughly 250 or more. The family test in `domain-strategy-and-duplicate-content.md`
  still passes — each tier's page set is a strict superset of the one below, so
  no two families serve the same pages — but the two contemplative families go
  on serving identical page sets, as they already do, and `ranksAt` keeps doing
  its job there.

## Readings taken, flagged for confirmation

- **"Visible from" is read as inclusion, not linking.** The young-adult edition
  *carries* the children's pages at their normal URLs, in its own navigation and
  reading plan, exactly as the canonical site does; it does not merely link out
  to undercover-pets.com. The narrower reading (a cross-link or a "see also")
  would leave every edition's page set as it is and change nothing a reader can
  see, which is not what the sentence says. **Confirm.**
- **The chain is strict and the two contemplative families sit together at the
  top.** Fellowship of Light and the Communion of the Called each see
  everything; they differ, as now, by framing and by their orders' own codex
  topics. Nothing in the direction ranks one contemplative order above the
  other. **Confirm.**
- **"Additional POV scenes for any chapter" is read as an engine feature that
  does not exist yet:** a `::: pov` block (or a whole `::::: scene`) inside *any*
  chapter of *any* thread, marked as contemplative-tier, which renders only on
  contemplative builds and is simply absent — not a placeholder — everywhere
  else. Today a POV block's visibility follows its character
  (`isCharacterPovIncluded`), and a chapter is in or out whole. The smallest
  faithful mechanism is a tier attribute on the container
  (`::: pov brother-fintan tier=contemplative`) mirrored in the chapter's
  `povs:` list, honoured by the chapter renderer, the scene-POV pages and the
  View-from buttons; the markdown-containers test would pin it. **Confirm the
  reading; the mechanism is mine to draft.**
- **Such a scene is an overlay, and the self-containment rule climbs the ladder
  with it.** The children's rule already says a child reading only the pet blocks
  must be able to follow what happened. Read upward: every tier's reading of a
  chapter must be complete without the blocks the tier above adds, so a
  contemplative POV scene may deepen, reflect on, or witness a chapter, but no
  lower tier may depend on it for the plot. This matches the Season 8 posture
  already in force — devotional narrative kept beside the record, never
  load-bearing, no `canon_facts`. **Confirm.**

## Left open — indexed in `open-questions.md`

- **The canonical site.** `fianilchruinne.com` and GitHub Pages hold everything
  public and deliberately hide the private `church-space` thread. Under the new
  ladder the contemplative editions become the only complete view. Is that the
  intended shape — canonical = general tier, contemplative = the whole — or
  should the canonical site now opt into the thread and stand at the top with
  them?
- **Hero casts and reading plans on the widened editions.** Each edition's cast
  and `readingPlan` were chosen against its narrow page set. A young-adult site
  that now carries the pet chapters, and a contemplative site that now carries
  the whole main sequence, may want their doors re-hung; this is framing, and it
  is Dermot's taste each time.

## What happens next

Nothing in the registry moves on this intake alone: re-pointing an edition is
draft-and-stop in `CLAUDE.md`, and the readings above want a yes first. On
confirmation the work is one PR for the ladder in `lib/editions.js` with its
validator and tests, the reader-profile and domain-strategy notes updated to
match, and a second PR for the contemplative POV marker.
