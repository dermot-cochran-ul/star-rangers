## The direction — a dangerous Teddy Bear

Dermot, verbatim:

> A dangerous Teddy Bear, actually a Pandoid, soft and warm, but never weak or
> foolish.

No attachment, no slot, no thread named.

### What it says, in one line

One character: a Pandoid whom someone has mistaken for a toy — soft and warm
being true, weak and foolish being the toy's implication and false.

### What canon already fixes about any Pandoid character

All from `intake-2026-07-26.md`'s four rulings of 16 August and the Fliade
entry drafted on them:

- Sapient; descended from a bear-like Fliadan lineage, nothing terrestrial
  behind them; *Pandoid* and *bear-like* are the observers' words.
- Large, powerfully built, upright, thick grey fur, quiet over broken ground.
- **Not spacefaring.** So the character is on Fliade, or has been taken off
  it — and the second would be an abduction under the FTP sapience clause,
  which is a season, not a character page.
- **The Archive's record is observational.** No Pandoid has given an account
  of themselves; no name, no speech, no contact. The Fliade entry says *the
  Pandoids did not come to anyone; they were arrived at.*
- The endonym is a marked absence, deliberately loose.
- The ladder is refused: nothing about them is a level.

### Readings taken, flagged for confirmation

- **"Teddy Bear" is the observers' word, not the record's.** The third
  hurried naming on Fliade after *Anstolik* and *Pandoid*, and one step
  further in: reindeer and bears are animals, a teddy bear is a toy. The draft
  makes that step the page's subject. **Confirm that the name is a nickname
  the record marks as such, and not what the character is.**
- **The character is on Fliade, met by the survey, observationally.** The only
  placement that keeps every ruling above intact. The draft puts them at the
  upper cavern levels, where the surface party already works and the Anstolik
  range, never in the deep networks the survey declines to enter. **Confirm.**
- **"Soft and warm" read literally** — fur and the deep's geothermal warmth,
  measured by the party's thermal instruments — and then as the toy's
  implication, which the page refuses. **Confirm.**
- **"Dangerous" read as the tone rule requires** — hinted, never shown. The
  draft states once what a person of that mass could do at that range with
  the party's back turned, and records that nothing was done. **Confirm that
  no harm is done on the page, and that none is planned.**
- **"Never weak or foolish" read as competence shown by placement, not by
  speech.** The character never speaks (canon: no contact). The one act on
  the page is moving a survey relay to the party's own furthest mark. **Confirm
  that a wordless act is the right register for the first individual Pandoid.**
- **Pronoun: *they*, marked as the record's refusal to claim one.** The party's
  log says *he*, kept as the party's word. The per-species pronoun rule
  (7 August) says a Pandoid system, if any, is declared in the species entry;
  none is, so the translation default applies and the page says so. **Confirm.**

### What the draft commits the story to

Beyond the rulings above, all extensions to the Fliade entry:

1. **Individual Pandoids are distinguishable to the survey, and one is smaller
   than the adults.** Age is not fixed — the page strikes *young* explicitly,
   because the survey has no method for ageing a Pandoid.
2. **A Pandoid has watched the party unseen, at close range, more than once.**
   The eleven-minute thermal log at the edge of the working light.
3. **A Pandoid has moved a survey instrument to the party's own furthest
   mark.** Two readings filed (courtesy / a line drawn), neither chosen. This
   is the one that changes Fliade: it means the Pandoids are aware of the
   survey and one of them has acted on it. The Fliade entry's *they were
   arrived at* still holds — nothing here is contact, nothing is speech —
   but the asymmetry has been answered from the other side, once, without a
   word. **This is his to confirm; the PR stops on it.**
4. **Survey Lead Hesper Nakagawa and Instrument Specialist Toma Egede** (the
   Anstolik codex, 2832 UCSD) reused as the survey's two voices. Consistent
   with the codex; no new named survey personnel.
5. **No unit placed past the line since; the party has not crossed it.** A
   standing survey practice, small, reversible in prose.

### What stays open — three questions, put as choices

**1. Where the character lives.**

- **(a) The Fliade survey record, as drafted** — a character page in the
  observational register, no chapter. Keeps every ruling. *Recommended*,
  because it is the only placement that costs nothing settled.
- **(b) The alien-protagonists strand** (4 September, fourth direction, open).
  The Pandoids were named there as one of three species settled enough to
  carry a season. A Pandoid ensemble on Fliade, seen from inside, would be the
  first Pandoid account of themselves — which the Fliade entry currently says
  the Archive does not hold, so it is a canon change to that page's framing,
  not just new prose.
- **(c) The children's tier.** *Teddy bear* is a child's word and Undercover
  Pets is the children's thread; but that thread is animal, not alien, and a
  Pandoid is a person. Would need a route to Eden that does not exist.

**2. The page title.**

- **(a) *Teddy*, as drafted**, with *the Teddy Bear* and *Individual Four* as
  aliases — the party's word, marked as the party's on the first line.
- **(b) *Individual Four*** as title, *Teddy* as alias — the log's neutrality,
  which the draft argues is not neutral either.
- **(c) An endonym.** Not available: the record holds none, and inventing one
  is the absence-filling the doctrine refuses.

**3. Size.**

- **(a) Smaller than the adults, age unfixed, as drafted.**
- **(b) A cub** — commits the record to Pandoid young, to their being brought
  or coming near the party, and to what the adults at the margin are doing
  about it. Richer and much more expensive.
- **(c) A small adult.** Closes the question the other way; nothing on the
  page needs it closed.

### Direction check

An **extension**: nothing in the Fliade entry is altered, the observational

## Second, same session — where Pandoid characters belong

Dermot, verbatim, while the Teddy PR (#711) was in CI:

> Should Pandoid characters be in the child reader tier, maybe as an edition
> parallel to Undercover Pets?
>
> or their own thread?

A question, not a direction; put back as choices. What already bears on it:

- **A thread is the unit either way.** An edition is a filter over threads
  (`lib/editions.js`), so an edition *parallel to Undercover Pets* needs a
  thread to show, exactly as undercover-pets.com shows Season 2. Undercover
  Pets is both, and was a thread first.
- **The tiers nest** (3 September): children ⊂ young adult ⊂ general ⊂
  contemplative. A children's-tier thread is inherited upward and reaches
  the general reader by construction; a general-tier thread does not reach
  down. So placing a Pandoid thread with the child reader costs the general
  reader nothing.
- **Aliens were placed with the general reader** (3 September, fifth
  direction, life-stage reading) and the 4 September direction asks for at
  least one alien-protagonist thread or strand. Season 10's Chthonari strand
  is serving that. The Pandoids were named there as one of three species
  settled enough to carry a season.
- **Not spacefaring.** A strand inside Undercover Pets is closed: no route
  to Eden. Any Pandoid storyline is on Fliade.
- **The Fliade entry's framing.** *What the Archive holds about Fliade is
  observational … nothing here is an account the Pandoids gave of
  themselves.* A thread told from inside is the first such account, so that
  sentence becomes dated rather than false — and how the record came to hold
  a Pandoid interior is the contact story itself, which under *explain gaps
  in-story* is the thread's own first job.
- **The life-stage rule** makes a children's-tier Pandoid protagonist young
  in that tier's years, which decides the intake's third question (size)
  toward *a cub*, and commits the record to Pandoid young.
- **The endonym.** A thread from inside must call the people, and the
  individuals, something. *Pandoid* is the survey's word and cannot be
  theirs. That is a canon vocabulary decision — registered in the species
  entry like a pronoun system — and his, before a line is drafted.
- **Mind design.** The 4 September trope rule: what each mind must be, stated
  before drafting. The Chthonari got `mind-design.md`; the Pandoids have the
  four rulings and the *whether their memory works as ours* question left
  deliberately open. A Pandoid section there comes first.
- **An edition needs a domain and a face**, the same two things the alien
  edition (3 September) is waiting on; subdomains are approved when domains
  run out.

### Three shapes, put as choices

1. **Their own thread, at the children's tier, on Fliade, from inside —
   edition to follow.** *Recommended.* The direction's own register — soft
   and warm, a protagonist a child wants to hold who turns out to be a
   person, *never weak or foolish* as the lesson — is the children's tier's,
   and the asymmetry (*someone arrived at your home and never asked*) is a
   story a child understands whole. Reaches every tier by nesting. Costs: a
   season, a mind-design section, the endonym, Pandoid young, and the Fliade
   sentence dated in-story. The edition (subdomain, face, `editions.js`)
   follows when the thread can fill a site, the alien edition's own logic.
2. **Their own thread at the general tier.** Satisfies the 4 September
   direction a second time; does not reach the child reader; competes with
   the Chthonari strand for the same shelf. Fewer commitments about young.
3. **Stay in the survey record — no thread.** Teddy as drafted and any
   further Pandoids as observational character pages, the Fliade entry
   untouched. Costs nothing settled and gives the child reader nothing.

Not offered: a strand inside Undercover Pets (no route to Eden), or an
edition without a thread (nothing to show).

The Teddy page holds under all three: it is the survey's side, and a thread
from inside would supply what the page marks absent rather than contradict it.
