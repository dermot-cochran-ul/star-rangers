## The direction — the shadow at the edge

Dermot, verbatim:

> A lingering shadow tries to invade Tobble's noogenic universe by assuming
> Tissadelle's appearance and form. but behaves in a way that the real
> Tissadelle never would have.

(The full stop before *but* read as a comma — the typo-flagging convention
applied; no other reading changes the sense.)

Attached without comment, and read as reference for the shadow's borrowed
appearance: two generated stills of a red-haired woman with the hair worn
loose, in knotwork and jewellery and a dress cut to be looked at, against a
glowing knot and circuitry; and a 25-second clip of the same woman in a navy
coat with gold stars worked onto the cloth, cut short, walking a candlelit
chamber for a camera. Parked in `reference-art/` — the two stills at full
size, the clip as a five-frame strip — and recorded under *Images* in
`images.md`. Nothing ships.

### What it commits, as read

Three things, and the third is the one that makes it a scene:

1. **Tobble's noögenic universe exists and can be approached.** This is the
   settled arc answer (28 July: the protouniverse is centred on Tobble; the
   terminus draft, approved 27 August, has the founding), so the direction
   sits after the terminus and does not reopen it. "Invade" reads as
   *seek admission to* — the edge is a boundary, and the thing is on the
   wrong side of it.
2. **Something wears her shape.** Appearance and form: surface. The
   direction does not say what the thing is, and the draft does not either.
3. **It behaves as she never would.** This is the direction's whole
   mechanism and it is already the record's: the Unfounded entry holds that
   a counterfeit gets the shape right and the membership wrong, and that
   the tell is never the performance but the tracking. Her page is written
   almost entirely in what she declines, which means the corpus already
   carries the negative of every line the shadow will speak.

### Canon check

**The shadow has a name in the record, and only one.** The Noögenic Seeding
System's third operational constraint: *failed compression events may
produce nonviable shadow-seeds and local ontological disturbance*; the Seed
Mind Lifecycle lists *malformed shadow-seed output* among Phase 7's risks.
Dock Seven was a compression under catastrophic conditions, and what it
produced was viable only in part — *some of her, not her* (`s05e02c03`).
A nonviable shadow-seed of that event is the plain reading of "a lingering
shadow", and it is a reading the record has been holding open since the
constraint was written. The draft files it that way *presumptively* and
claims no more, which is what Tobble's idiom and the Archive's would both
do.

**"Shadow" has a second meaning in the record, and it does not fit.** A
Membrane Shadow is gravitational bleed from a mass that never crosses; it
*cannot act on this side, at all, under any circumstance*. The direction's
thing tries to get in, so it is not that, and the draft does not use the
term. The entry's other line does transfer: *it looked like someone I know*
is an observation about the witness first, and Tobble applies it to
himself before he looks.

**The behaviour is the Unfounded entry, exactly.** Counterfeit of a founded
shape; false empathy — warmth pitched at where you were standing a moment
ago, comfort that arrives fluent and off by one; cannot be addressed
because nobody performs the reading; cannot be destroyed, only starved,
because its structure is borrowed against a host's purpose. Whether a
shadow-seed *is* an Unfounded pattern the record does not say and the
draft does not decide; the entry's own doctrine is that the names record
how a counterfeit runs, not a kind it belongs to, and this one runs as the
entry describes.

**The counter is on the page already, twice.** The Gilded Saint's feeding is
closure-prevention disguised as consolation — *the homecoming that
resolves* — and the counter is shown-not-healed (`s07e01c02`); the
institutional form of the same feeding is closed by naming the debt in full
(`s07e01c03`). The shadow says *I came home*, which is the false homecoming
by name, and asks the station to reopen the file, which is the story
wanting to stay open. Tobble and Wender each apply the counter they were
taught. Nothing new is needed.

**The test is Órla's.** `s06e01c01`: *you're up early*, and watch whether
the answer comes back the way it comes from a person or from a room that
has been waiting to be asked. The family trade is the only test in the
corpus that cannot be passed by knowing the answer, and the draft hands it
to the one member who was on the step when it was first used.

**Every tell is a line from her page, negated.** She does not volunteer
assessments; the shadow announces itself. She does not perform confidence;
it says *I'm certain*. She corrects *cat* once, precisely, in front of him;
it says *there's my cat*. She never decides what a thing meant for anyone;
it says *you did all this for me*. She read the book twice and did not ask;
it asks what is under the glosses. She did the arithmetic twice; it will
not. She lived in the house on loan and handed it over clean; it says *it's
mine*. She was always formally dressed and never dressed to be looked at;
it arrives in the questing layer's dress and later in a coat with rank on
the cloth — which, since 4 September, canon says the Corps never puts
there.

**Costume, and the tone rule.** The two stills and the clip are glamour
shots. The draft uses them as the shadow's costume precisely because they
are what she would never wear, and the prose does not describe either
garment beyond what the finding needs: the dress is under a gloss in the
book (propriety, per the 27 August ruling), and the record keeps the gloss.
The rank-on-cloth reading of the clip rests on *Reading Rank at a Glance*
(`star-rangers-command-hierarchy.md`): the tab as the single mark, muted,
never on the garment.

**Tobble as detector.** The Unfounded entry: animals detect counterfeits
more reliably than instruments or people, because a creature that declines
without needing a reason is reading the membership rather than the shape.
Tobble is a person whose identity is a cat. The draft lets him decline
before he has a reason and then go and get the reasons, and lets the reader
find the entry; nobody points at it.

**No new physics.** A shadow-seed is canon; starving is canon; the tracking
tell is canon; a protouniverse has an edge because a membrane is
universe-scale. Everything the shadow does inside the draft is POV
experience or instrument reading. The one instrument-side claim — that a
secondary signature at the founding site registers at Threshold — rests on
the founding signature having registered there in the terminus coda.

## Readings taken, flagged as choices

Per the 29 August rule, put as shapes. The draft is written so that the
first three are all compatible with it, and commits to none.

**What the shadow is** — three shapes:

1. **A nonviable shadow-seed of the Dock Seven compression** (recommended,
   and the draft's presumptive filing). The record's own term; ties the
   thing to the event that made him founder; "lingering" is literal — it
   has been there since the compression and only now has an edge to stand
   at. Costs nothing.
2. **An Unfounded pattern that found the founding** — a Gilded-Saint-mode
   counterfeit borrowing against his want, unrelated to Dock Seven except
   as the occasion. Fits the behaviour equally; loses the tie to the
   compression.
3. **The unpreserved remainder** — the lost fantasy layer, or what was in
   it, arriving in her shape: the museum or ghost Tobble refused to make,
   made anyway from outside. The costume points here (it wears what was
   under a gloss). This is the gravest reading and the one that would
   make the lost members' question live in a new way; it is also the one
   the Unfounded doctrine says to be slowest to grant, since a counterfeit
   of a lost thing is still a counterfeit.

**Whether it ever leaves** — two shapes: it dims out over an unnumbered
interval, or it stays at the edge indefinitely as a marked absence he keeps
watch on. The draft ends with it still there and does not choose.

**The coda** — two shapes: keep the Wender scene (the shadow tries the
other door too, and the file staying closed is the arc's ethic tested one
last time), or cut it and let the scene be Tobble's alone. Recommended:
keep, on the ground that the corpus works in two threads and the shadow
asking to be *let in* on one side and to be *reopened* on the other is one
finding seen twice.

**Slot** — after the terminus, which has none. Open with it.
